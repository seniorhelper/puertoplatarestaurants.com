Two cruise ports, one city

Puerto Plata’s two cruise terminals handled most of the country’s cruise traffic in 2024: Amber Cove welcomed 1,201,255 passengers and Taíno Bay 994,609, together 82.6% of the 2.66 million cruise passengers who reached the Dominican Republic, as reported by Dominican Today from Ministry of Tourism figures.

Cruise visitors don’t know the town and have little time, so the restaurants that show a photo, opening hours and a readable menu are the ones they walk to.

What is the difference between Featured and Menu Pro?

Featured ($29 a month) gives you a top spot on Puerto Plata Restaurants with a badge, a photo, a pitch and your link. Will this guarantee more customers?

No, and we won’t pretend it does. We promise the work: a clearly labeled top placement, your photo and link, and with Menu Pro a menu that search engines can read and that we keep current. How many diners choose you still depends on your food, reviews and service.

How fast does it go live?

Yes. Tell us your regular hours and your cruise-day hours and we’ll show both clearly. With Menu Pro, a lunch menu built for port days can sit on its own section of your menu page, and we update it each quarter as the cruise season changes.
