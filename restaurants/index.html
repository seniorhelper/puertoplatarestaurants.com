How this list works

Every place here appears in public sources such as the Dominican Republic’s official tourism site, guidebook publishers, local directories and major review platforms. We link those sources on each card, describe the restaurant in our own words, and note when a detail comes from reviewers rather than the business. Hours and prices change too often to publish reliably, so we leave them out; check the restaurant’s own page or call ahead.

Five places from our earlier version were removed or corrected because we could not confirm them or because they are reported closed. The details are on our editorial policy page. Know a place we should add? Tell us.
