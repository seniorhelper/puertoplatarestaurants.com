Privacy policy

This page explains what data Puerto Plata Restaurants and its partners handle when you visit. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. If you use the contact form, the details you type (name, email, message) are delivered to us by the FormSubmit service so we can reply. Our tools (meal picker, calculators) run in your browser; choices you make there are not sent to us. Some tools remember your picks on your own device using local storage, which you can clear at any time.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at www.aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads are used, as Google requires.

Maps and fonts

The map only loads when you press "Show map". It then requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap and the Leaflet library from cdnjs, which receive your IP address as any web request does. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact page.
