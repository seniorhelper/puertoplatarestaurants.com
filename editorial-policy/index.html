Editorial policy and corrections

How we research, what we refuse to publish, and what we removed.

This is how Puerto Plata Restaurants decides what to list and how we describe it.

What makes the list

  • The place must appear in at least two independent public sources, such as its own website or social page, the national tourism board, a guidebook publisher, or a major listing platform. Where we only found one, the listing says so in our data file.
  • We record every source link and the month we checked it, shown as "Last verified".
  • Descriptions are written in our own words from what the sources say. Phrases like "reviewers mention" mean the detail comes from public reviews, not from us.

What we never publish

  • Star ratings, review counts or "best seller" claims that we did not collect ourselves.
  • Hours or prices unless the business itself publishes them, and even then we suggest checking.
  • Made-up dishes, chefs or awards.

Editor's tags

Tags such as "romantic", "sea view" or "casual" help the meal picker. They are our judgment based on the cited sources (for example, a beachfront location or a candlelit room described by a guidebook), not paid labels.

Photos

Food photos on cuisine and area sections are representative. We do not present them as a listed restaurant’s food.

Maps

We only pin coordinates that we can verify against OpenStreetMap or an official geocoder. Where we could not, the listing links to an OpenStreetMap search instead of guessing.

Updates

We re-check listings at least every quarter. Closed places are removed and logged below.

Recent removals and corrections

We keep this log so readers and search engines can see what changed and why.

  • Mares Restaurant & Lounge: Removed: reported permanently closed by Wanderlog and Restaurant Guru listings (checked October 2026). Earlier card also mislabeled it as waterfront.
  • Cacao & Soul: Removed: no trace of a business by this name in any directory, map or travel source (October 2026).
  • El Pescador del Malecón: Removed: no restaurant by this name found in any source (October 2026).
  • El Rincón Criollo: Removed: no Puerto Plata restaurant by this name found; the only matches are in other countries (October 2026).
  • Portofino Puerto Plata: Removed: no Puerto Plata restaurant by this name could be confirmed (October 2026).
  • La Isabela by the Sea: Corrected: the real restaurant is La Isabela Colonial Restaurant in a Victorian house downtown, not by the sea. Unverified claims (tasting menu, named sourcing) removed.
