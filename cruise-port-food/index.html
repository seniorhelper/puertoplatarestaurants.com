Cruise day

Where to eat near the Puerto Plata cruise ports

Puerto Plata has two cruise terminals. Which one you dock at decides whether lunch is a walk or a ride.

Short answer

Taíno Bay is in the city: the historic center and Malecón are about a 10–15 minute walk, and a free trolley has been reported. Amber Cove is about 12 km (7.5 miles) west, roughly 20 minutes by car, so plan a taxi or excursion for an independent lunch.

Docking at Taíno Bay

Taíno Bay sits on the edge of downtown. Cruise travelers describe walking into town in well under fifteen minutes, past Umbrella Street to the central plaza and back along the Malecón. That puts every historic-center and waterfront restaurant within reach.

A four-hour plan

  1. Walk to the central park and the cathedral; coffee or an early lunch at a Victorian house such as Casita Azul or Kaffe.
  2. See the Amber Museum on Calle Duarte or Fort San Felipe at the end of the Malecón.
  3. Stroll the Malecón and stop at La Carihuela Beach or Tam Tam for fried fish and tostones.
  4. Head back with time to spare; all-aboard times are strict.

Docking at Amber Cove

Amber Cove is a separate terminal west of the city with its own shops, pools and restaurants inside the port. To eat in town you need transport: the port has a transportation hub with taxis and rental cars. On the way into the city you pass the Cofresí and Costámbar areas, where Los Tres Cocos and Charros CaribeMex are options for a lunch stop.

A five-hour plan

  1. Taxi from the port into the historic center (agree the fare first).
  2. Walk the old town and the Malecón, with lunch at a waterfront or Victorian-house restaurant.
  3. If you want a view, the cable car up Pico Isabel de Torres has operated since 1975; check it is running that day.
  4. Return by taxi at least an hour before all-aboard.

Cruise-day tips

  • Money: carry small pesos or small US bills. Bills in restaurants usually add 18% tax and a 10% service charge; our tip helper shows the total.
  • Timing: lunch service is busiest from noon to 2 p.m. Eat early if your ship leaves mid-afternoon.
  • Water: drink bottled or purified water.
  • Hours change: many places close one day a week, so have a backup on the list.
