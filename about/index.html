About Puerto Plata Restaurants

Who runs this guide, how it is paid for and how we keep it accurate.

Puerto Plata Restaurants is an independent guide to eating out in Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic. It is published by Eye To Ad Media, a small digital publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We are not owned by any restaurant, hotel or tour company.

Every listing on this site links to the public sources we used, carries a "last verified" month, and is described in our own words. We do not publish star ratings or review counts, because we did not collect them ourselves and they go stale quickly. Instead each listing links out so you can read current reviews.

Some businesses pay to be featured. Those placements are always labeled Sponsored, their links carry rel="sponsored", and payment never changes what we write about other places.

How to reach us

Spotted a closure, a wrong address or a place we should add? Use the contact page. We read every message and correct mistakes quickly.
